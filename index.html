Stefan Alexandre – Career Journey (2019–2025)

2019 – Amateur Beginnings: Stefan Alexandre’s basketball journey began in Poland, competing in amateur and semi‑professional leagues. His natural athleticism and versatility quickly set him apart, earning attention from structured teams.

2020 – AZS Vistula University: Stefan progressed to play in the Warsaw Academic Championships – I League with AZS Vistula University, under the guidance of Coach Dariusz Sworst. That season included intense league battles, showcasing discipline, strategy, and tactical maturity.

2022 – Professional Development (2 Liga): Stefan joined the AZS UW Warsaw team coached by Konrad Budka. Under his mentorship, Stefan refined his game in Poland’s competitive 2 Liga — a turning point in his professional development.

2023 – International Squad & Promotion Contender: Returning to Vistula, Stefan competed with one of Poland’s most international academic teams. His leadership caught the eye of nationally ranked pro teams pursuing promotion.

2025 – Costa do Sol (Mozambique): Stefan returned home to play professionally with Costa do Sol, one of Mozambique’s top clubs, bringing his international experience, skill, and competitive edge back to the national stage.

Mentored by Coaches Dariusz Sworst and Konrad Budka, Stefan’s foundational training emphasized fundamentals, conditioning, tactical strength, and professional habits — helping him rise from beginner to international pro.

From amateur court battles in 2019 to pro competition in 2025, Stefan Alexandre’s journey reflects resilience, adaptability, and elite growth.
